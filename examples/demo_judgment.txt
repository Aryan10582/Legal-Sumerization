SYNTHETIC INTERFACE DEMONSTRATION — NOT A BENCHMARK CASE
The appellant challenged the dismissal of an application for repayment of a deposit. The agreement required written notice before termination. The respondent argued that the appellant had waived repayment by accepting the final account.
The court considered the notice provision and the correspondence between the parties. It found that accepting the account did not waive the express right to repayment. The respondent had not established any contractual exception allowing retention of the deposit.
The appeal was allowed. The order dismissing the repayment application was set aside, and the matter was remitted for determination of the amount payable. No order was made as to costs.
