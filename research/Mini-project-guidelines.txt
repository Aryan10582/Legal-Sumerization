Mini-Project Guidelines – 2026 
1. Submission of Problem Statement 
Each team must identify a real-world problem and submit the problem statement on or before Lab 04. 
The problem statement document should include the following: 
• Title  
• Introduction: Write in own words with 150 to 200. 
• Keywords: At least 4 to 5 words. 
• Objectives: At least two to three. 
• Data Source: Mention the reference 
• References:  
a) Include 20–25 references from SCOPUS- or Web of Science-indexed journals. 
b) Avoid citing conference papers, textbooks, blogs, websites, or other non-peer-reviewed sources. 
c) Preferably use recent journal articles published from 2020 onwards. 
d) Ensure that all references are cited appropriately in the text and formatted according to the IEEE 
referencing style. 
• Names and registration numbers of all team members 
Team Size: Three students per team. 
2. Mini-Project Evaluation and Report Submission 
During the mini-project evaluation, each team must submit a detailed project report containing the following 
sections: 
• Title 
• Abstract 
• Keywords 
• Introduction 
• Literature Survey 
• Objectives 
• Data Source 
• Methodology 
• Implementation Details 
• Results and Discussion 
• Conclusion and Future Scope 
• References 
Front Page Requirements 
The front page of the report should contain: 
• Name of the School 
• Name of the College and its logo 
• Project Title 
• Names of the Team Members 
• Roll Numbers 
• Registration Numbers
